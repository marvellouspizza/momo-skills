# Twelve-Section Blueprint

Generate exactly 12 complete Narrative Sections by default. Loading/intro is required but not counted.

## Required Intro

Use NM-003 preloader, NM-002 click-to-start, and NM-011 sound gate. The user starts the experience before the long scroll narrative begins.

## Section 1: Threshold Object

Purpose: establish the brand/theme, giant title, reference object, and first viewport.

Modules: NM-001, NM-004, NM-005, NM-006, NM-007, NM-012, NU-001, NU-002.

## Section 2: World Premise

Purpose: state the world condition or tension that makes the story matter.

Modules: NM-005, NM-015, NM-019.

## Section 3: Material Close-Up

Purpose: move close to the reference object and reveal its material character.

Modules: NM-004, NM-006, NM-007, NM-008, NM-020.

## Section 4: Light / Ingredient Emergence

Purpose: introduce the first emotional or material system: light, ingredient, scent, data, craft, energy, or texture.

Modules: NM-015, NM-017, NM-018, NM-019.

## Section 5: Spirit / Core Claim

Purpose: make the central promise. Let the object enter a stronger state.

Modules: NM-010, NM-015, NM-020.

## Section 6: Sound / Sensory Layer

Purpose: add sound, rhythm, ambient, touch, ritual, or sensory depth.

Modules: NM-011, NM-017, NM-019.

## Section 7: Invitation Inside

Purpose: invite user interaction. Pointer, hover, or scroll response becomes more obvious.

Modules: NM-008, NM-012, NM-015, NM-020.

## Section 8: Beyond Words

Purpose: show that the story is an experience system, not only copy or claims.

Modules: NM-007, NM-018, NM-015.

## Section 9: Spark Transformation

Purpose: transform the visual mood. Shift color, object state, speed, or atmosphere.

Modules: NM-010, NM-016, NM-017, NM-020.

## Section 10: See / Feel / Hear / Interact Map

Purpose: present 4-7 hoverable anchors such as features, ingredients, cases, rituals, or touchpoints.

Modules: NM-009, NM-012, NM-018.

## Section 11: Principles / Ritual Steps

Purpose: provide concrete substance: 3-4 principles, steps, features, or proof points.

Modules: NM-009, NM-013, NM-015.

## Section 12: Dark Contact / Final Release

Purpose: final high-contrast scene with contact, CTA, and footer reveal.

Modules: NM-010, NM-013, NM-016, NM-017, NU-007.
